## Timeline

The study consists of two parts in which you are involved:

1) A **14 day exploratory diary study** using the T1MESCULPTURES tool

2) A **30 minute (max) interview** within two weeks of the conclusion of the diary study about the results and your thoughts in more depth

### 1. Diary Study

You will use [the tool](README.md#An-app-for-time-to-space-conversion) over the course of **two weeks** to **turn time into space at least 10 times**.


> [!NOTE]
> You can obviously create more within the period! The study won't be dismissed just because you like the medium :D

#### Goal

The goal is to have tried to **create a T1MESCULPTURE at least 10 times within 14 days**.

This explicitly does NOT mean to have 10 finished pieces you would want to exhibit! If you end up with that, great! But **each iteration counts**, fails and frustrations are just as important as success stories.

You can, of course, share your results with me. I'm happy to see them! But for the studies, the diary and additional surveys are important. Please **fill out and submit a [_sculpture log_](#sculpture-log) after each try**.

> [!NOTE]
> WHAT'S A TRY?
> Every time you would "close" the program - _because you are happy with the result, maybe want to refine the animation, are frustrated and done, or any other reason_ marks the end of a try.
> Also, once you replace the image sequence with a new one.
> But I'm pretty sure you will know what reasonably can be called a try for you and the study :)

#### Surveys / Logs

The surveys / logs are an integral part of the diary study.

They are your digital diary.

One survey is mandatory, one is a nice-to-have and one is an offer from me to you.

Try to invite me into your mind! I know this is a tough ask, but **the surveys will be the basis for the interview afterward**. So remember that **I don't necessarly have to understand everything, I will ask you to explain things I did not understand.**

**Just, if it's okay with you, share your emotions with me.** Your anger and frustration, your joy and happyness, your artistic vision, your pragmatism and everything in between.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Feel free to fill out the forms in German as well. As my thesis is in English, all of the materials are, aber ich kann ja auch deutsch :)

##### Sculpture Log _(mandatory after each try)_

The sculpture log is your feedback loop **after each try**.

What went wrong, how did it feel, etc. But not every field of the form is mandatory.

You can find the sculpture log here:

> [!TIP]
> Feel free to use bullet points, abbreviate, or find other ways to not have the log feel like a burden. That is not the goal and I don't want to disrupt your flow.

##### Eureka Log _(nice to have)_

This is your log to **tell me about (happy) incidents**.

_Something finally "clicked", the resulting sculpture is in no way how you imagined it, you found a different way to approach the medium, "time" or something else?_

I would love to hear about it. If you find the time, fill out the short survey.

You can find the eureka log here:
https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdpAtH1LuoQJPaEP8Uz4s7WbqXqRtJkO1Omy2z0hDwEZIcslQ/

##### Translation Plan _(an offer from me)_

The translation plan is like a **"sculpture planner"**. The various field are **designed to help you use time as a medium and form the right approach** for whatever you would want to convey in your animation and sculpture.

It resembles a digital piece of paper, you can **draw** and **place pictures** anywhere, **enter text** and "scan it", so I can see a bit of how your mind works.

This plan is how my mind approaches these sculptures, but so far, I have been the only one making sculptures of time, so I can't know if it helps you. I just hope!

I would **recommend you try the translation plan at least twice**, sometime in the beginning of the study and once near the end, but I will not make you.

You can find the translation plan here:
https://t1mesculptures.maltehillebrand.de/study/translation-plan
